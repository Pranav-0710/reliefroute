Lessons in memory

    How ReliefRoute works

    1. Report. Field workers send text, a photo or a voice note in any language from /report. Gemini turns it into requests: place, item, quantity, urgency. The same need reported twice becomes one request.
    2. Plan. A coordinator clicks Dispatch wave. The agent assigns every waiting request to free trucks with its best learned strategy (a neural policy trained with reinforcement learning), on real road distances that avoid reported floods.
    3. Drive. Each driver opens their truck's link on a phone, follows the road route and marks each stop delivered, failed or road blocked.
    4. Learn. A blocked road becomes a hazard that the next routes avoid, unserved relief goes back in the queue, and every finished route teaches the agent: field scores, an ETA correction and lessons for the planner.

    No coordinator account? Open the Simulator tab and click Run 5 episodes to watch the agent plan, hit a collapsed bridge, recover and learn.

    How ReliefRoute works

    1. Report. Field workers send text, a photo or a voice note in any language from /report. Gemini turns it into requests: place, item, quantity, urgency. The same need reported twice becomes one request.
    2. Plan. The agent assigns waiting requests to free trucks on real road distances that avoid confirmed floods, with the strategy that has delivered best in the field so far. A coordinator dispatches, or turns on the autopilot: the agent then proposes a wave by itself when an urgent request arrives or something changes, and the coordinator approves or rejects it.
    3. Drive. Each driver opens their truck's link on a phone, follows the road route and marks each stop delivered, failed or road blocked. When a road is cut, every truck heading into it is rerouted from where it is, and its driver sees the new route.
    4. Learn. Unserved relief returns to the queue, a hazard reported twice is confirmed, and every finished route teaches the agent: field scores that pick the next strategy, an ETA correction, and lessons, including the reasons a coordinator gives for rejecting a plan.

    No coordinator account? Open the Simulator and run 5 episodes to watch the agent plan, hit a collapsed bridge, recover and learn. Other agents can use ReliefRoute's tools over MCP at /mcp.
